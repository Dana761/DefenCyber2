# B14 — Teach Your Friends About a Cybersecurity Topic

## Activity 1 — Teaching Phishing and Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- **Friend:** Amanda  

### Findings
- Amanda initially believed phishing only occurred through fake emails  
- Explained that phishing can also occur through WhatsApp, SMS, and social media  
- Discussed examples of fake login pages used to steal credentials  
- Learned how to identify suspicious links and urgent messages  
- Understood the importance of changing passwords after credential exposure  
- Learned how Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds an extra security layer  
- Used the UWA login system as a real-world example of 2FA protection  
- Recognized that passwords alone are insufficient for account security  

### Outcome
Amanda showed improved understanding of phishing attacks and stated she would be more careful with suspicious links and login requests.

## Activity 2 — Teaching Public Wi-Fi Security Risks

- **Friend:** Michael Evan  

### Findings
- Michael initially believed public Wi-Fi was generally safe  
- Explained that attackers can intercept traffic on insecure networks  
- Discussed fake Wi-Fi hotspots used to trick users  
- Learned the importance of HTTPS for secure browsing  
- Understood risks of accessing banking or email accounts on public Wi-Fi  
- Discussed practical protection steps:
  - Avoid sensitive logins on public Wi-Fi  
  - Check for HTTPS connections  
  - Avoid suspicious networks  
  - Use mobile data for important activities  

### Outcome
Michael understood the risks of public Wi-Fi and stated he would be more cautious when using public networks in the future.
