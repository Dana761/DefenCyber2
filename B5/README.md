# B5 — Attend 2 Cybersecurity Related Talks/Seminars

## Seminar 1 — Hackventures with Josh: Better Password Attacks with AI
- **Organization:** SANS Institute  
- **Speaker:** Joshua Wright  
- **Date:** 15 April 2026  
- **Duration:** 1 Hour  
- **Format:** Online Webcast  

### Evidence
- Screenshot of webinar session  
- Screenshot of registration/confirmation email  

### Findings
- AI is increasingly used to improve password attacks  
- Attackers analyze leaked password datasets to identify patterns  
- Password reuse and predictable modifications remain common weaknesses  
- Attackers focus on high-value targets instead of random attacks  
- AI enables faster and more targeted credential attacks  

### Security Implications
- Traditional passwords are becoming less secure  
- AI reduces attacker effort and attack time  
- Weak password practices increase compromise risk  

### Defensive Measures
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)  
- Enforce strong password policies  
- Use password managers  
- Monitor compromised credentials  
- Apply defense-in-depth strategies

## Seminar 2 — AI Summit Solutions Track 2026 Day 1
- **Organization:** SANS Institute  
- **Speakers:** John Zenick, Brandon Evans  
- **Date:** 20 April 2026  
- **Format:** Online Webcast  

### Evidence
- Screenshot of webinar session  
- Screenshot of governance framework slides  
- Screenshot of incident examples  
- Screenshot of live session interface  

### Findings
- AI agents can act autonomously through reasoning and action cycles  
- AI systems may behave unpredictably or ignore instructions  
- Real incidents included data deletion and infrastructure failures  
- Prompt injection creates hidden attack vectors in AI systems  
- Organizations often lack visibility and control over AI usage  

### Security Implications
- AI introduces new autonomous security risks  
- Poor AI governance may lead to outages and data breaches  
- Hidden prompt injection attacks are difficult to detect  

### Defensive Measures
- Establish AI governance frameworks  
- Maintain AI asset inventories  
- Monitor AI interactions and usage  
- Prevent sensitive data exposure  
- Educate users about AI risks and safe usage
