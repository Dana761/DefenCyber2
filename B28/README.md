# B28 – Produce a Cyber Safety Flyer for University Students

## Objective
Create and distribute a cyber safety awareness flyer for university students.

## Target Audience
University students

## Tools Used
- Canva

## Description
A cyber safety flyer titled:
“DON’T GET HACKED AT UNI!”

was designed to educate university students about common cybersecurity risks.

Topics included:
- Strong passwords and 2FA
- Phishing awareness
- Safe downloading practices
- Online privacy and digital footprint
- Device security

The flyer used a clean and student-friendly design with short and practical advice to improve readability and engagement.

## Implementation
- The flyer was designed using Canva.
- A printed version was produced.
- The flyer was shared with two university students.
- The content was briefly explained to ensure understanding.

## Impact
The activity helped raise cybersecurity awareness among peers.

Students were able to understand:
- The importance of enabling 2FA
- Risks of phishing emails
- Dangers of downloading untrusted software

This demonstrates that simple visual communication can effectively teach cybersecurity concepts to non-technical users.

## Reflection
This activity showed that cybersecurity awareness can be improved through clear communication and audience-focused design. Sharing the flyer physically also increased engagement and discussion among students.

## Skills Developed
- Cybersecurity awareness communication
- Risk identification and simplification
- Visual design and information structuring
- Audience-targeted messaging
